# Micro-benchmarks 2026-11-06-9b65e04e2

porto vs asyncio (default loop) and asyncio+uvloop, `bench/micro/` (53-benchmarks.md §2, 11-task-model-and-trap-protocol.md §11, ADR-034). Times are pyperf means per operation; a ratio is baseline time ÷ porto time, so 2.0× means porto is twice as fast. Each baseline column shows the fastest gating variant; *poll-free* is `uvloop-readyfd`, uvloop without the empty-kevent cost of an idle poll.
